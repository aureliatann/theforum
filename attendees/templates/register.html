What is The Forum?

The Forum is an annual talk show that provides a platform for Indonesian students to engage with experts and public figures on issues related to Indonesia's politics, economy, and society.

This Year’s Theme

Prosperous Indonesia: Advancing Beyond Wealth

“Without the dynamic participation of the economic sector, we cannot have growth and prosperity. Without growth, we cannot alleviate poverty, create relevance, or provide employment.” — President Prabowo Subianto

Indonesia's economy is growing, but millions are being left behind. The Forum 2026 tackles the critical question: How do we turn economic resilience into shared prosperity? Join us as we explore pathways to reduce inequality and build a future where growth benefits all Indonesians, not just a select few.
